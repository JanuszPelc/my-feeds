<h2>Martin Fowler Publishes Essay on Uncanny Valley of LLM Voice</h2>
<a name="story-25880"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic-cdn.cars24.com%2Fprod%2Fcms%2F2026%2F01%2F29%2F0066df65-477d-4821-a782-2ab1c46a8c2eIMG_20260129_145754_289.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Martin Fowler examines the phenomenon of "LLM Voice," a distinct stylistic tone found in prose generated by large language models that triggers a textual uncanny valley and erodes reader trust. He argues that this artificial prose style is instantly recognizable to human readers, rendering automated detection tools largely unnecessary.</p><p>While advising authors to avoid using LLMs for drafting or rephrasing to preserve their authentic voice, Fowler acknowledges necessary exceptions, such as assisting individuals with Parkinson's disease or aiding non-native speakers with translation. He emphasizes that writing is fundamentally a tool for human thinking, warning that delegating prose generation to AI risks outsourcing comprehension itself.</p>

<h2>Lexifina Proposes Git-Style Version Control for US Code</h2>
<p>Legal technology company Lexifina has developed a version-control system for the United States Code, allowing users to track legislative amendments in a manner similar to software source code management. By replaying historical amendments sequentially across nine code titles from 2017 onward, the project successfully reproduced 361 out of 365 changes to match official release points.</p><p>The system aims to address challenges in legal research and language model accuracy, which often falter when handling temporal validity and statutory evolution. While the tool successfully maps effective dates and amending laws for most changes, limitations remain regarding retroactive reach-back provisions and pattern-matching constraints for effective-date notes.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://lexifina.com/blog/git-for-the-us-code">lexifina.com</a> | <a href="https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50268380">hacker news</a></small></p>

<h2>IPv4 Address Allocation Mapped to Physical Land Area</h2>
<a name="story-25910"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fvad.solutions%2Fipmap%2F2023%2F2023-ipv4-72dpi.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>A hypothetical visualization redistributes global landmass based on each country's proportion of the world's IPv4 addresses. The model calculates a country's area by multiplying its share of locatable IPv4 addresses by the total world land area.</p><p>The calculation excludes Antarctica and approximately 70 small territories to maintain scale. The methodology utilizes IP Trust geolocation data and focuses on countries with at least 100,000 IPv4 addresses and 20,000 km² of land.</p>

<a name="story-25872"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftechcrunch.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2Freddit-daus.jpg%3Fw%3D680&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>

<h2>US Battery Storage Installations Reach Record 20.2 GWh in Q2 2026</h2>
<a name="story-25844"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Felectrek.co%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fsites%2F3%2F2026%2F06%2FGreen-River-Energy-Center-Utah.jpg%3Fquality%3D82%26strip%3Dall%26w%3D1600&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>The United States added a record 20.2 gigawatt-hours of battery storage capacity in the second quarter of 2026, bringing total installations for the first half of the year to 30.8 GWh according to a report from the Solar Energy Industries Association and Benchmark Mineral Intelligence. Growth was overwhelmingly driven by utility-scale projects, which accounted for approximately 18 GWh of the quarterly total, led by heavy deployment in Arizona, Texas, and Utah.</p><p>While utility-scale and commercial segments expanded rapidly to meet rising grid demand and data center constraints, residential battery storage experienced a 27% year-over-year decline in Q2 following the removal of the federal 25D residential clean energy tax credit. Driven by stronger-than-expected demand, Benchmark raised its cumulative storage forecast through 2030 by 11.5% to 683 GWh, with projected total installations for 2026 reaching 71 GWh.</p>

<h2>Carmelite Monks Use CNC Stone Carving for Gothic Architecture</h2>
<a name="story-25739"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcowboystatedaily.imgix.net%2FMonks-sculpting-12.29.24.JPG%3Fixlib%3Djs-3.8.0%26q%3D75%26auto%3Dformat%2Ccompress%26fit%3Dclip%26w%3D2048&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>The Carmelite Monks of Wyoming are utilizing Computer Numerical Control stone-carving machines to construct a traditional Gothic monastery. By employing multi-axis equipment from Prussiani Engineering, the monks produce complex architectural elements like pinnacles and window sills in a fraction of the time required by manual carving.</p><p>The digital workflow incorporates 3D modeling and computer-aided manufacturing programming, enabling members trained in computers to contribute to the construction effort. The robust machinery allows for high-precision reliefs and automated overnight processing, bridging ancient architectural ambitions with modern manufacturing technology.</p>

<h2>Mistral AI Releases Open Weights for Mistral Large 4 Model</h2>
<a name="story-25820"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fntwenmyhsoimhczyxtgl.supabase.co%2Fstorage%2Fv1%2Fobject%2Fpublic%2Fmedia-derivatives%2FMDA_c7ef42b25f47%2Fweb-1080.jpg%3Fv%3D1792226325&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
