<h2>Codwaves Releases Quance Resonance Suppressor Plugin</h2>
<a name="story-25697"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic.kvraudio.com%2Fi%2Fb%2Fquanceui.png&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Codwaves has released Quance, a real-time resonance suppression plugin for Windows and macOS available in VST3, AU, and AAX formats. Utilizing dynamic spectral processing, the plugin detects harsh frequency peaks and reduces them while incorporating dedicated tone and transient protection systems to preserve harmonics and vocal formants.</p><p>Quance includes features such as an intelligent learn function for automatic focus point placement, six source modes, up to eight focus points, and five quality settings ranging from zero-latency live monitoring to high-resolution mixing and mastering modes. The plugin is available at an introductory price of $49.99 until November 30, 2026, alongside a 7-day trial on the developer's website.</p>

<h2>Kazrog Releases Vac Rac TSL Tube Optical Compressor Plugin</h2>

<p>Kazrog's Vac Rac TSL is a plugin emulation of the Inward Connections Vac Rac tube optical compressor/limiter, developed alongside designer Steve Firlotte. The software models four hardware configurations: the TSL-5, TSL-4, TSL-3, and the transformerless TLM-1.</p><p>The plugin features adjustable THD for tube saturation, sidechain filters, and Analog Entropy for modeling channel-to-channel component tolerance. It is compatible with macOS, Windows, and Linux in VST2, VST3, AAX, AU, and LV2 formats.</p>

<h2>Art DSP Releases Canvas 2.0 3D Visual Mixing Plugin</h2>
<a name="story-25590"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmusictech.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F10%2FCanvas-2-her%402000x1500.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Art DSP has released Canvas 2.0, a visual mixing plugin for macOS and Windows that allows producers to position audio tracks as movable objects within a virtual three-dimensional room. The software maps stereo positioning left-to-right, frequency content vertically, and volume along the forward-backward axis, letting users adjust their mix directly inside the 3D environment rather than relying strictly on standard mixer controls.</p><p>The plugin includes spectral sidechaining, a built-in EQ editor, integrated reverb control, and one-click mixing templates to process entire tracks. Canvas 2.0 is available now for $79 or through a rent-to-own plan, with plugin formats supporting VST3, AAX, and AU.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://musictech.com/news/gear/art-dsp-canvas-2/">musictech.com</a> | <a href="https://www.artdsp.net/canvas">source</a></small></p>
