<h2>AudioBrain Releases ChimeBrain Physically Modeled Wind-Chime Instrument for macOS</h2>
<p>AudioBrain has released ChimeBrain, a physically modeled wind-chime instrument designed for macOS. Instead of relying on prerecorded samples, the software calculates the vibrations of suspended metal bars in real time to generate sound for music production, scoring, and sound design.</p><p>The instrument features up to 36 individually configurable metal bars with adjustable dimensions, materials, and pitch registers, alongside MIDI performance options, sweep and shake gestures, and direct on-screen interaction. It also includes five virtual microphone models and optional room acoustics, with version 1.0.2 disabling room reverb by default on new instances to reduce CPU usage. ChimeBrain is available as a universal AU, VST3, and standalone plugin priced at $19.</p>

<h2>Wide Blue Sound Updates Humanizer Pro to Version 1.0.6</h2>
<p>Wide Blue Sound has released version 1.0.6 of Humanizer Pro, a MIDI effect plug-in designed to introduce realistic timing and velocity variations to digital tracks. <h2>OBS Studio 33.0.0 Released with Updated Browser Engine and Plugin Overhaul</h2>

<h2>Pine DSP Releases Blox Rack Modular Effects Plugin</h2>
<p>Pine DSP has released Blox Rack, a modular effects plugin available for Windows and macOS in VST3, AU, and AAX formats. The software features six individual processors—an equalizer, compressor, chorus, delay, reverb, and exciter—that can be arranged across nine slots, re-ordered, duplicated, or run in parallel within a custom signal chain.</p><p>The plugin includes a horizontal top panel equipped with a spectrum analyzer for detailed equalization, alongside an extensive modulation system featuring four LFOs, an envelope follower, an XY pad, and six macro controls. Blox Rack ships with over 500 tagged presets, and purchasing a full license grants access to all six constituent processors as standalone plugins.</p>
