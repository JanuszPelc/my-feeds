<h2>Nvidia Backs Indian AI Startup Simplismart in $14 Million Series B Round</h2>
<a name="story-25546"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimg.etimg.com%2Fthumb%2Fwidth-650%2Cheight-488%2Cimgsize-66644%2Cresizemode-75%2Cmsid-131164392%2Ftech%2Ffunding%2Fchipmaker-nvidia-in-talks-to-lead-20-million-round-in-gen-ai-startup-simplismart%2Fsimplismart-team.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Nvidia has participated in a $14 million Series B funding round for Bengaluru-based AI startup Simplismart, valuing the company at approximately $100 million. Dallas Venture Capital led the investment, with additional participation from Accel, Shastra VC, and Micromax. Simplismart develops software that sits between AI models and underlying computing infrastructure, automatically configuring deployments to balance speed, cost, and GPU utilization for enterprise customers.</p><p>The startup’s platform helps businesses optimize performance and reduce GPU serving costs, with clients including video-generation platforms InVideo and HeyGen. Simplismart has experienced rapid growth, scaling its annualized revenue run rate 14-fold over a six-month period and generating more than half its revenue from the United States. Alongside the funding, Nvidia is collaborating with Simplismart on technical integrations involving its Dynamo inference software to expand market adoption.</p>

<h2>Intel to Acquire Israeli Quantum Computing Startup Classiq</h2>
<a name="story-25521"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdnimg.co%2F0adf2a70-1ef7-4f05-b8a4-faa1afd3c764%2Fscreenshots%2F794ca51b-b8a4-47bd-a763-e1661de1a3d0%2Fquantum-computing-startups-quantum-platform.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Intel Corp. has agreed to acquire Tel Aviv-based Classiq Technologies Ltd., a quantum computing software startup, to accelerate the development of a full-stack quantum computing platform. Classiq has raised more than $200 million in funding and maintains a workforce of approximately 130 employees.</p>

<h2>California Startups Surpass Prior US Annual Funding Records in 2026</h2>
<a name="story-25455"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fnews.crunchbase.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2Fconcentrated-capital.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>California-based startups have secured more than three-fourths of all U.S. venture funding in 2026, pulling in an unprecedented $385 billion across seed- through growth-stage rounds.
