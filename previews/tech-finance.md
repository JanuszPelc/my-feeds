<h2>Ramp Investors Joe Lonsdale and Keith Rabois Pushed Lawmakers to Probe Airwallex Following Scrapped Deal</h2>

<h2>Blackstone-Led CoreWeave Loan Sparks Chip-Backed Debt Boom</h2>
<p>A 2023 private credit loan of $2.3 billion led by Blackstone Inc. to CoreWeave Inc. established a model for financing artificial intelligence infrastructure using graphics processing units as collateral. This strategy has catalyzed a rapidly expanding chip-backed debt market projected to approach $1 trillion.</p><p>The initial financing arrangement has reshaped modern corporate credit by demonstrating how specialized tech assets can secure massive debt facilities. As lenders and borrowers continue to scale these GPU-backed facilities, the trend is introducing new risk profiles and transforming how capital is deployed across the tech sector.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-23/blackstone-bet-on-chips-drove-a-debt-boom-that-s-upending-credit">bloomberg.com</a></small></p>

<h2>Baba Secures $40 Million Series A Funding for AI Medicare Platform</h2>

<h2>Taler Operations AG Founded to Operate GNU Taler in Germany</h2>
<p>Taler Operations AG is being established in Bochum, Germany, to operate the privacy-focused GNU Taler payment system in Euros. The newly formed company will apply to BaFin, the German financial supervisory authority, for an electronic money institution licence after the project's initial banking partner, GLS Bank, decided not to pursue the role.</p><p>GNU Taler remains free and open-source software, with the new corporate entity focusing strictly on managing the regulated payment infrastructure. To satisfy regulatory capital mandates and fund operations prior to revenue generation, Taler Operations AG is currently seeking investments of at least €50,000 from backers aligned with the project's privacy and public-interest goals.</p>

<h2>Microsoft and Alphabet Object to Tokenized Stock Trading on OKXICE Platform</h2>
<p>Microsoft and Alphabet have joined 14 other public companies in formally objecting to a plan by OKXICE—a joint venture between crypto exchange OKX and NYSE parent ICE—to offer round-the-clock tokenized trading of their shares. The companies utilized a Securities and Exchange Commission framework that allows qualifying firms to block third-party tokenized stock listings within a 30-day notice period.</p><p>The pushback highlights growing corporate caution toward digital asset trading, driven by concerns over potential pricing discrepancies and the loss of traditional market closures used for digesting earnings reports. While individual companies can block their shares from specific platforms using the regulatory exemption, the mounting objections threaten to strip the planned venture of a significant portion of its initial stock lineup.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://fortune.com/2026/10/21/microsoft-alphabet-object-blockchain-versions-shares-veto/">fortune.com</a></small></p>
