<h2>Nevis Raises $70 Million Series B Funding for Wealth Management AI Platform</h2>
<p>Nevis, a New York-based wealth management technology startup founded by former Revolut executives, has raised $70 million in a Series B funding round led by EQT Ventures. The round brings the company's total funding to $110 million and values it at $500 million, with participation from new investors including Charles Schwab Corporation and QuantumLight.</p><p>The company provides artificial intelligence software designed to automate routine administrative tasks and paperwork for financial advisors. <h2>California Startups Surpass Prior US Annual Funding Records in 2026</h2>
<p>California-based startups have secured more than three-fourths of all U.S. venture funding in 2026, pulling in an unprecedented $385 billion across seed- through growth-stage rounds. This figure exceeds the total amount raised by all U.S. startups in any prior single year on record, driven largely by massive megarounds for artificial intelligence pioneers based in San Francisco.</p><p>Major financing rounds for companies like OpenAI and Anthropic account for more than half of the statewide total, while other substantial contributions come from mature firms such as Waymo and Databricks as well as newer ventures like Prometheus and Safe Superintelligence. Additionally, early- and seed-stage California companies have captured nearly $62 billion, representing about 57% of all U.S. early-stage funding this year.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/california-startups-most-us-funding-2026-openai-anthropic/">news.crunchbase.com</a></small></p>

<h2>Blackstone-Led CoreWeave Loan Sparks Chip-Backed Debt Boom</h2>
<p>A 2023 private credit loan of $2.3 billion led by Blackstone Inc. to CoreWeave Inc. established a model for financing artificial intelligence infrastructure using graphics processing units as collateral. This strategy has catalyzed a rapidly expanding chip-backed debt market projected to approach $1 trillion.</p><p>The initial financing arrangement has reshaped modern corporate credit by demonstrating how specialized tech assets can secure massive debt facilities. As lenders and borrowers continue to scale these GPU-backed facilities, the trend is introducing new risk profiles and transforming how capital is deployed across the tech sector.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-23/blackstone-bet-on-chips-drove-a-debt-boom-that-s-upending-credit">bloomberg.com</a></small></p>

<h2>Baba Secures $40 Million Series A Funding for AI Medicare Platform</h2>

<h2>Mave Raises $28 Million Series A to Automate Threat Investigations</h2>
<p>Cybersecurity startup Mave has raised $28 million in a Series A funding round led by Red Dot Capital Partners, bringing its total capital raised to $34 million. <h2>Taler Operations AG Founded to Operate GNU Taler in Germany</h2>
<p>Taler Operations AG is being established in Bochum, Germany, to operate the privacy-focused GNU Taler payment system in Euros. The newly formed company will apply to BaFin, the German financial supervisory authority, for an electronic money institution licence after the project's initial banking partner, GLS Bank, decided not to pursue the role.</p><p>GNU Taler remains free and open-source software, with the new corporate entity focusing strictly on managing the regulated payment infrastructure. To satisfy regulatory capital mandates and fund operations prior to revenue generation, Taler Operations AG is currently seeking investments of at least €50,000 from backers aligned with the project's privacy and public-interest goals.</p>

<h2>Andreessen Horowitz Rebrands a16z Crypto to a16z Frontier</h2>
<p>Andreessen Horowitz has rebranded its digital assets division, a16z Crypto, to a16z Frontier. The rebranding expands the mandate of its $2.2 billion fund to include artificial intelligence, robotics, and other emerging technologies alongside its existing blockchain focus.</p><p>Managing partner Chris Dixon stated the shift reflects the blurring distinction between crypto-native companies and other technology sectors. While the firm will continue to support crypto startups and advocate for regulation, the expansion aligns with recent strategic shifts by other prominent investors such as Paradigm and Haun Ventures.</p>
