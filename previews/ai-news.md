<h2>GPT-6.2 Astra Identifies Creator of Anonymous 1808 Sketchbook</h2>
<a name="story-28501"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsubstackcdn.com%2Fimage%2Ffetch%2F%24s_%21mr1c%21%2Cw_1456%2Cc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%3Agood%2Cfl_progressive%3Asteep%2Fhttps%253A%252F%252Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%252Fpublic%252Fimages%252F85c196c7-1cc6-440f-b086-de25bf788ac1_1672x941.png&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra model identified the author of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale University’s Beinecke Library in approximately 22 minutes. By analyzing publicly available primary and secondary sources, the model hypothesized that the drawings were created by Louis-Guy-Charles-Guillaume de Rohan-Chabot, a minor French aristocrat and British Army officer. The AI successfully directed the author to a specific microfilmed diary at Cleveland State University, which confirmed the identity through matching handwriting, consistent travel itineraries, and specific descriptions of landmarks.</p><p>The discovery demonstrates the AI's emerging ability to conduct sophisticated research in non-deterministic fields like history, moving beyond purely mathematical or logical tasks. While the model exhibited idiosyncratic and riddle-like prose—potentially a side effect of training optimizations for formal logic—it successfully navigated obscure references, including a 2014 PhD thesis, that were unlikely to be surfaced by human researchers. This suggests a significant acceleration in the speed and scope of historical discovery using large language models to synthesize disparate, unindexed archival data.</p>

<p>AWS launched Strands Decider v2, a suite of five open decision models for AI agents built on Google’s Gemma 4 architecture. These models, available under the Apache 2.0 license, range from 2 billion to 31 billion parameters. Unlike generative models, these "decision models" are designed to process a given state—such as an agent trace or support ticket—and a fixed set of possible answers to return a probability for each option rather than generating free-form text.</p><p>The v2 release features a bidirectional attention architecture, enabling multiple questions to reuse a single encoded state to reduce latency and cost. Benchmarks indicate that the 31B model achieves an average score of 82.1 across six public text benchmarks, outperforming Google’s Gemini Decision 1. Although not yet integrated into Amazon Bedrock, the models have been used internally at Amazon to replace Claude Sonnet 4.6 for ticket labeling, resulting in significantly lower operational costs and faster processing times.</p>

<h2>Cisco and OpenAI Launch Shirudo to Protect Infrastructure Against AI Cyberattacks</h2>
<p>Cisco and OpenAI have launched Shirudo, an open-source infrastructure shield designed to autonomously defend systems against AI-driven cyberattacks. Developed as part of the Secure Infrastructure Project, Shirudo utilizes extended Berkeley Packet Filter (eBPF) technology to deploy cryptographically-signed shields in milliseconds. This mechanism allows infrastructure to block critical exploits in real-time, providing a layer of protection before official software patches can be distributed.</p><p>The initiative addresses the shrinking "mean time to exploitation," where AI models can discover and weaponize software vulnerabilities faster than human security teams can respond. By integrating OpenAI’s Codex agent to autonomously generate and validate mitigation code, the partners aim to transition from reactive security to "hardened by default" infrastructure. This approach focuses on securing workloads at runtime to create systems that are immutable and can be quickly reverted to a known good state in the event of an attack.</p>
<p>Nov 24 · <a href="https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/23/cisco-and-openai-launch-shirudo-an-open-source-infrastructure-shield-to-protect-against-ai-driven-cyberattacks/">siliconangle.com</a> | <a href="https://newsroom.cisco.com/c/r/newsroom/en/us/a/y2026/m11/cisco-and-openai-launch-secure-infrastructure-project-to-safeguard-agentic-ai.html">newsroom.cisco.com</a></p>
