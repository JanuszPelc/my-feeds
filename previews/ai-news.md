<h2>Anthropic Introduces FlashProve to Verify AI Model Identity</h2>

<p>Anthropic has released FlashProve, an open-source research prototype designed to provide cryptographic proof that a specific AI model produced a given output. Anthropic has released technical specifications and working code as part of this ongoing research effort.</p>
