<a name="story-25727"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftheoutpost.ai%2F_next%2Fimage%2F%3Furl%3Dhttps%3A%2F%2Fcdn.theoutpost.ai%2Ffiles%2Farticle_419434_Pq_Au_K9f_J_Fgbjg_Nb_Y_Kyk_S_Nc_1200_80_1771963433596_e336c64d6b.jpg%26w%3D1080%26q%3D75&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Valve has addressed concerns among independent developers regarding AI-generated game clones on Steam, stating that the platform will not act as a gatekeeper against AI tools.
