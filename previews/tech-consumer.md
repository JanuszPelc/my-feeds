<p>Microdramas—serialized, vertical short-form videos lasting a minute or two—have rapidly expanded into a multibillion-dollar global streaming market, generating $11 billion in worldwide revenue in 2025. Regulatory challenges in China and consumer protection concerns in various jurisdictions have emerged around aggressive marketing, unclear pricing, and automatic renewals, while the increasing adoption of artificial intelligence in production threatens to drive down costs further while impacting traditional labor.</p>
