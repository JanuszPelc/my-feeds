<h2>Samsung Expects 2026 Capital Expenditures to Rise 60% Amid Strong AI Demand</h2>
<a name="story-25775"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sammobile.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2024%2F02%2FSamsung-HBM3E-12H-DRAM-Chip.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Samsung Electronics plans to increase its capital expenditures by roughly 60% this year to approximately $63 billion, driven largely by massive investments in chip manufacturing infrastructure. The company reported that its high-bandwidth memory capacity is completely sold out through 2027, backed by 10 long-term supply agreements secured with major customers.</p><p>The surge in spending comes as Samsung navigates a recovering semiconductor market propelled by artificial intelligence demand, even as its recent chip profit missed broader analyst estimates. The vast majority of the capital expenditure is earmarked for its Device Solutions division to expand advanced memory production capacity and secure long-term competitiveness.</p>

<h2>Meta Attributes No Kings Instagram Suspension to Claude AI Activity</h2>
<a name="story-25800"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fsubstackcdn.com%2Fimage%2Ffetch%2F%24s_%21O48g%21%2Cw_2400%2Cc_limit%2Cf_auto%2Cq_auto%3Agood%2Cfl_progressive%3Asteep%2Fhttps%253A%252F%252Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%252Fpublic%252Fimages%252F13f8e3bf-e4c3-45c9-846b-4b9537ed181e_1496x523.png&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Meta temporarily suspended the official Instagram account of the No Kings Coalition during a nationwide voting and protest campaign, initially blaming high-frequency posting before altering its explanation. Company executives subsequently claimed that security systems detected automated reading behavior from a Claude AI tool operating at an elevated volume, which triggered a mistaken enforcement action.</p><p>Technical experts and critics have questioned Meta's shifting justifications, noting that reading and publishing activities are technically distinct and that automated traffic spikes are common during major political mobilizations. The incident underscores ongoing concerns regarding corporate transparency and the impact of automated content moderation on political communication during critical electoral periods.</p>

<h2>AI Demand Shifts Flash Memory Market Power from Apple to Suppliers Like Kioxia</h2>
<a name="story-25772"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.lsvisionhd.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F03%2FMemory-Price-Surge-in-Q1-2026-Driven-by-Supply-Disruptions-and-AI-Demand-1024x572.webp&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Surging demand for flash memory from artificial intelligence data centers has caused prices to quintuple, significantly altering negotiating leverage in the semiconductor industry. For years, Apple held dominant bargaining power, routinely compelling flash-storage manufacturers to accept steep discounts for its devices.</p><p>However, the rapid expansion of AI hyperscalers has absorbed available chip supply and driven up costs, leaving suppliers such as Kioxia with newfound market power. This supply-demand imbalance highlights how infrastructure investments in artificial intelligence are reshaping pricing dynamics and supply chains across major consumer electronics manufacturers.</p>

The investigation seeks to determine if the company's market dominance is being used to disadvantage competitors.</p>

<h2>Google Expands AI Mode Ads Globally</h2>
<a name="story-25755"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimages.seroundtable.com%2Fgoogle-expands-ai-mode-ads-worldwide-d1maw1YX.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Google is expanding its AI Mode advertising to international markets and launching multi-turn ads in the United States. <h2>Reddit Introduces &#x27;For You&#x27; and &#x27;Following&#x27; Feed Options</h2>
<a name="story-25711"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstatic0.makeuseofimages.com%2Fwordpress%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2022%2F07%2Freddit-hide-post.jpg%3Fq%3D50%26fit%3Dcrop%26w%3D825%26dpr%3D1.5&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Reddit is rolling out a redesigned feed interface featuring separate "For You" and "Following" tabs, shifting its homepage toward an algorithmic recommendation model similar to TikTok and other social platforms. The "For You" feed blends posts from subscribed communities with tailored recommendations, while the "Following" feed displays only content from joined communities.</p><p>The global rollout follows prior testing phases that drew mixed responses from users who preferred a purely subscription-based home feed. Reddit states the update aims to foster community discovery and active participation, and users can switch between feeds with the platform retaining their last-viewed tab.</p>

Foundry currently serves defense and commercial markets by producing complex electro-mechanical products where traditional manufacturing faces constraints.</p>

<h2>Dropbox Plans 9% Workforce Reduction and Technology Chief Departure</h2>
<a name="story-25700"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.tradersunion.com%2Fimages%2Fregional-news%2Fcustom-by-date%2F2026%2F10%2F28%2Fsmall%2F3999712_768.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Dropbox announced plans to reduce its global workforce by approximately 9%, affecting about 200 jobs, as part of an effort to align investments with its primary product and growth priorities. The cloud-based storage company expects to incur between $27 million and $33 million in severance and related cash expenditures, primarily accounting for these costs in the fourth quarter of 2026.</p><p>Alongside the structural changes, the company stated that Chief Technology Officer Ali Dasdan will step down from his role on November 18, 2026. Despite the workforce reduction and leadership transition, Dropbox indicated that its third-quarter financial guidance remains in line with or above previously provided ranges.</p>
