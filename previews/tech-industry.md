<h2>Proton to Re-Interview Staff on AI Usage to Determine Continued Employment</h2>

<h2>Instagram Tests 90-Minute Horizontal Videos for Creators and Podcasters</h2>
<p>Instagram is launching a test allowing select U.S. creators to upload horizontal videos of up to 90 minutes. <h2>Venkatesh Rao Publishes Essay on Cognitive Mercantilism and AI Panic</h2>
<p>In his essay "Cognitive Mercantilism," Venkatesh Rao argues that current societal anxieties around artificial intelligence structurally resemble the mercantilist worldview that preceded modern capitalism. He contends that contemporary critics treat intelligence as a scarce, fixed resource to be hoarded by elites, much like early modern economists viewed land and bullion.</p><p>Drawing parallels to Joyce Appleby’s history of capitalism, Rao suggests that abundant AI will shift the economic paradigm from cognitive production to consumption. Just as historical agricultural advances allowed populations to become consumers rather than solely manual laborers, AI-driven abundance could spark a "cognitive industrious revolution" where ordinary individuals leverage vast intelligence for new ambitions and projects.</p>

<h2>Volkswagen and Xpeng Secure Approvals for Autonomous Driving Trials in China</h2>

<h2>Microsoft Offers Voluntary Buyouts to Roughly a Quarter of Its Workforce</h2>
<p>Microsoft is offering voluntary exit buyouts to approximately 60,000 employees, or roughly a quarter of its global workforce, spanning eligible staff at the vice president level and below across the United States, Canada, India, and the United Kingdom. Participating divisions include gaming, Windows, Copilot, human resources, and finance, with deadlines for acceptance set for early December.</p><p>The initiative builds on earlier workforce reduction efforts and a voluntary retirement program introduced earlier in the year as the company seeks to control headcount and adapt team structures.
