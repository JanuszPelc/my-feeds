<h2>Felix Rieseberg Discusses the Obsolescence of Coding Interviews and Career Advice for Junior Engineers</h2>
<a name="story-25635"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.licdn.com%2Fdms%2Fimage%2Fv2%2FD4D10AQFEAag77I1JkA%2Fimage-shrink_800%2FB4DZpeGuhZGwAg-%2F0%2F1762515413669%3Fe%3D2147483647%26v%3Dbeta%26t%3DRvk2lKwgjnx2TYSgq6kSITRnhcXIV_9mfxarxu8h2Ss&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Felix Rieseberg, a hiring manager at Anthropic, has stopped using traditional coding interviews because AI tools like Claude now outperform human candidates on standard coding challenges. He argues that this technological shift diminishes the traditional advantages of accumulated framework knowledge and tenure, creating new opportunities for engineers without established social capital or status.</p><p>The essay advises junior engineers to prioritize business value over backlog maintenance, bypass slow organizational processes by building working prototypes over a weekend, and leverage their fresh perspectives to challenge assumed technical impossibilities. Rieseberg notes that rapid AI execution enables newcomers to pitch and ship impactful ideas much faster than previously possible.</p>

<h2>SK Hynix Reports Record Third-Quarter Profit and Revenue Driven by AI Demand</h2>
<a name="story-25636"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.reuters.com%2Fgraphics%2FSK%2BHYNIX-RESULTS%2Fbyprnblwzpe%2Fchart.png&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>SK Hynix reported record third-quarter 2026 financial results, posting revenue of 104.6155 trillion won (approximately $78.8 billion) and an operating profit of 83.3338 trillion won (approximately $62.76 billion). Both figures significantly surpassed analyst expectations, driven by surging global demand for high-bandwidth memory chips used in artificial intelligence applications.</p><p>The company's quarterly sales surpassed 100 trillion won for the first time, with operating profit surging over sixfold compared to the previous year. SK Hynix also announced that shipments and revenue for its upcoming HBM4 chips are expected to outpace HBM3E products, backed by long-term agreements covering more than half of its production capacity for 2027 and 2028.</p>

<h2>Marissa Mayer Criticized for Comments on Gender and AI in New York Times Interview</h2>
<a name="story-25564"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstorage.ghost.io%2Fc%2F0f%2F76%2F0f76b548-bc58-4f25-abc3-3f5ebca07da4%2Fcontent%2Fimages%2F2026%2F10%2FSan_Francisco_Chronicle_Archive_Image_23130584607860.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Former Yahoo CEO Marissa Mayer faced widespread public criticism following a New York Times profile in which she promoted her new AI assistant, Dazzle, and dismissed the ongoing relevance of gender bias in the technology industry. During the interview, Mayer revealed she used her AI tool to generate descriptive qualities for her young daughter, while asserting that passion acts as a gender-neutralizing force that erases systemic barriers.</p><p>Observers and industry reports quickly contrasted Mayer's remarks with stark professional disparities, including lower funding levels for female-founded startups, high attrition rates among women in tech, and recent high-profile setbacks such as the cancellation of the Grace Hopper Celebration. The backlash also follows broader federal rollbacks of diversity programs and research grants that have disproportionately affected women and early-career researchers in STEM fields.</p>
