<h2>Cerebras Details Deployment Architecture for Trillion-Parameter AI Models</h2>
<p>Cerebras details how it deploys multi-trillion-parameter frontier AI models across its wafer-scale WSE-3T chips using pipelining and memory management. Using the three-trillion-parameter open-source Kimi K3 model as an example, the company explains how model layers are distributed across multiple wafers to achieve high throughput.</p><p>Because each WSE-3T wafer contains 44 GB of on-chip SRAM, entire model layers fit directly onto individual chips, allowing model weights to remain stationary while activations stream between wafers via Ethernet RDMA. The pipeline handles concurrent requests using micro-batching across dense and linear-attention layers to maximize both inference speed and resource utilization.</p>

<h2>US Creative Industries Experience Over 200,000 Job Losses Amid AI Growth</h2>
<p>America’s creative and media industries have lost more than 200,000 jobs over the last four years, a downturn that coincides with the rapid expansion of generative artificial intelligence systems capable of producing text, images, and audio at scale. Although isolating exact job losses caused specifically by AI is complicated by broader industry trends such as market consolidation and shifts toward user-generated content, nearly all digital media subsectors have experienced persistent employment declines.</p><p>The essay compares this transition to previous technological disruptions, noting how internet file-sharing permanently altered music and publishing business models. While the music industry mitigated revenue losses by shifting toward live touring and concerts, sectors like film and television lack a similar live-event fallback, leaving traditional studio employment increasingly vulnerable to competition from automated and online content creation.</p>
