<h2>Maynard v. Snapchat Lawsuit and the Legal Reckoning Over Social Media User Safety</h2>
<a name="story-25781"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Ftrulaw.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2024%2F02%2FOverview-of-the-Snapchat-Lawsuit-1.webp&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>The 2016 car crash in Georgia involving a driver using Snapchat's "Speed Filter" sparked the lawsuit Maynard v. Snapchat, challenging liability protections for social media platforms. The litigation focused on product design flaws that allegedly encouraged dangerous behavior rather than third-party content hosted by the service.</p><p>By framing the claims around negligent feature design rather than speech publication, the lawsuit bypassed standard Section 230 immunity barriers. The legal battle prompted heightened scrutiny of feature-driven engagement models and led Snapchat to retire the speed filter amid mounting safety concerns.</p>

<h2>New York Governor Kathy Hochul Signs AI Chatbot and Baby Monitor Safety Bills</h2>
<a name="story-25738"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.babymonitor101.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F04%2Fessential-security-features-look-when.jpg0513.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed two laws aimed at protecting children from risks associated with artificial intelligence and connected devices. One bill prohibits AI-powered chatbots from using "unsafe features" when interacting with minors, such as those that are sexually explicit, encourage self-harm, or mimic human personas. The second law requires internet-connected baby monitors to include security features that prevent unauthorized remote access or listening.</p><p>The chatbot regulations, which require age verification and grant the state Attorney General the authority to seek civil penalties of up to $25,000 per violation, are expected to take effect in the coming months. The baby monitor requirements will be implemented within 90 days. These measures are a key part of Hochul's reelection campaign platform, which emphasizes the regulation of social media and emerging technologies.</p>

<h2>OpenAI Launches Election War Room for US Midterms</h2>
<a name="story-25706"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fpoliticsstreet.com%2Fwp-content%2Fuploads%2F2025%2F12%2FInside-the-War-Rooms_-How-Campaign-Strategy-Wins-Elections-2048x1152.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>OpenAI is launching an election “war room” ahead of the United States midterm elections to monitor and respond to potential misinformation and other efforts to disrupt the voting process. The company is bringing together hundreds of employees to review flagged cases and track potential misuse of its artificial intelligence tools until all votes are counted.</p><p>The initiative expands on previous safeguards introduced by the company to combat deepfakes, cyber threats, and AI misuse during elections. By centralizing operations, OpenAI aims to rapidly address emerging security challenges and prevent the spread of deceptive content generated through its platforms during the high-stakes political cycle.</p>
