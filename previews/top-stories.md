<a name="story-25693"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fa57.foxnews.com%2Fstatic.foxnews.com%2Ffoxnews.com%2Fcontent%2Fuploads%2F2026%2F02%2F1200%2F675%2Fmn-ice-plates-database-homeland-security-investigations.jpg%3Fve%3D1%26tl%3D1&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>

Initiated under National Security Presidential Memorandum 7 and funded through programs like Operation Keyhole, the multi-agency campaign utilized undercover agents, drones, and invasive digital tools typically reserved for counterterrorism and international crime.</p><p>Defense attorneys and former law enforcement officials compare the directive to the FBI’s historic COINTELPRO operations, noting the vast scale of data collection involving intercepted encrypted chats, financial records, and commercial data brokers. The filings indicate that the surveillance extended far beyond Minnesota to activists and conferences in cities across the United States as part of a broader federal crackdown on opponents of the administration's immigration policies.</p>

<h2>Apple Developer Tools on macOS Still Trust Compromised eMudhra Certificates</h2>
<a name="story-25676"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdeveloper.apple.com%2Fforums%2Fcontent%2Fattachment%2Fff2cd813-bb43-4cae-8984-e40beed9a952&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Developer tools bundled with macOS—including git, curl, and python3—continue to trust compromised eMudhra intermediate certificates more than a week after Apple was notified of the security incident. Additionally, the recent release of macOS 27.2 failed to address the issue and overwrote manual workarounds applied by users, leaving development environments vulnerable to man-in-the-middle attacks.</p><p>This trust flaw allows attackers to intercept HTTPS connections, tamper with repository clones and pushes, and capture sensitive credentials like GitHub tokens. While Apple has secured Safari and applications using its core networking framework, developers must manually remove the affected root certificates and reapply fixes after system updates until an official patch is issued.</p>

<h2>David Harvey Discusses Recently Proposed Sub-O(n log n) Integer Multiplication Algorithm</h2>
<a name="story-25157"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fimage.slideserve.com%2F1427678%2Finteger-multiplication-mit-notes-and-wikipedia1-l.jpg&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>Mathematician David Harvey has published a commentary examining a recently proposed integer multiplication algorithm that claims a complexity of o(n log n) bit operations. The analysis offers perspective on the new complexity claim and its relation to established theoretical limits in computational mathematics.</p><p>The discussion follows earlier milestones in the field, including Harvey and Joris van der Hoeven's 2019 algorithm achieving O(n log n) bit operations, which confirmed a long-standing conjecture by Schönhage and Strassen. The newly proposed approach pushes past that established boundary into sub-O(n log n) territory, prompting careful scrutiny of its methods and assumptions.</p>

<h2>Ellia Lebleu Discusses Wikipedia&#x27;s Ongoing Cleanup of AI-Generated Content</h2>
<a name="story-25600"><img src="https://wsrv.nl/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdown-id.img.susercontent.com%2Ffile%2Fid-11134207-7qul0-lg20jwjg48s790&amp;w=1200&amp;h=1200&amp;fit=inside&amp;we&amp;output=jpg&amp;bg=white" width="30%" align="right" /></a>
<p>In an interview with IEEE Spectrum, WikiProject AI Cleanup founding member Ellia Lebleu discusses the volunteer efforts launched in late 2023 to detect and remove artificial intelligence-generated material from Wikipedia. Lebleu notes that while early AI content was marked by glaring clichés and predictable structures, modern machine-generated submissions have become increasingly subtle, often featuring faulty citations and nuanced rhetorical patterns.</p><p>Despite the English Wikipedia's policy prohibiting large language models for content generation, volunteers estimate that AI text still accounts for a notable percentage of new drafts and submissions. The project relies on developing detection tools, automated verifiers, and coordination with the Wikimedia Foundation, raising broader questions about maintaining Wikipedia as a uniquely human-written corpus in an era dominated by generative AI.</p>
<p><small>Links: <a href="https://spectrum.ieee.org/wikipedia-wikiproject-ai-writing-cleanup">spectrum.ieee.org</a> | <a href="https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50257326">hacker news</a></small></p>
